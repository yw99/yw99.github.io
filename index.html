Yuxiao Wen

Yuxiao Wen (top)

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University, advised by Prof. Yanjun Han and Prof. Zhengyuan Zhou. My research focuses on reinforcement learning, online decision-making, and causal inference, with applications to digital advertising, recommendation systems, and adaptive experimentation.

My work develops statistically principled and sample-efficient methods for sequential decision-making, connecting theoretical guarantees with practical challenges in digital advertising, personalized recommendations, and AI agentic systems. I have also worked as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon Web Services and a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at Spotify, developing learning algorithms for large-scale advertising and recommendation systems.

More recently, I have been exploring reliable LLM post-training and evaluation under operational constraints.

I'm graduating in 2027 and seeking full-time roles!
