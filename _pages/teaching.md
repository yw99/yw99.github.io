### [DS-GA 3001: Applied Statistics](https://yanjunhan2021.github.io/courses/applied_stats/index.html)
{: .course-title}

Fall 2023, Fall 2024 · Graduate course, Center for Data Science · Instructor: Prof. [Yanjun Han](https://yanjunhan2021.github.io/)
{: .course-meta}

A rigorous introduction to classical models and tools in applied statistics, built around exponential families and generalized linear models, together with semiparametric and nonparametric methods.

Exponential families · Generalized linear models · Bootstrap · Survival analysis · Empirical Bayes · Average treatment effects · Doubly robust estimation · Nonparametric regression
{: .course-keywords}

### [MATH-UA 133: Mathematics for Economics III](https://math.nyu.edu/dynamic/courses/undergrad/math-ua-133/)
{: .course-title}

Spring 2024 · Undergraduate course, Department of Mathematics · Instructor: Prof. Trush Majmudar
{: .course-meta}

The third course in the Mathematics for Economics sequence, giving economics majors the tools for higher-level mathematics courses.

Vector calculus · Vector fields · Matrix analysis · Linear and nonlinear programming
{: .course-keywords}
